Design kit

Bo Meubles

Reference for the WordPress and Elementor Pro rebuild. The design system is the Intrio template by Designesia, unchanged. Everything below is read from the template's own stylesheets, so this page and the five mockup pages can never drift apart.

Colours

Template tokens. Accent values come from css/colors/scheme-01.css, surfaces and text from css/style.css. In Elementor these become Site Settings, Global Colors.

Carry this rule into the rebuild. The brand brown #805231 reads 5.59:1 on the cream surface, which passes, but only 2.24:1 on the dark surface #34241C. Anywhere it lands on a dark band it is lightened to #C89A6B (5.86:1). That override lives in css/bo-meubles.css and is the only colour change made to the template.

Contrast, WCAG AA

4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and meaningful icons. Semi-transparent template colours are resolved against the surface they sit on before measuring.

Typography

Headings and body share one family at --heading-font and --body-font. Headings are uppercase with negative tracking. See the build notes: the template's Google Fonts request is invalid, so a real face must be chosen and licensed at build time.
